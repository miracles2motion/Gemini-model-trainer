HOW COURTQUANT MARKET OPTIONS & FORMULAS WORK (1 TO 6 FORMULAS PER MODEL)
================================================================================
CourtQuant supports **5 live SportyBet basketball markets (including overtime)** plus **1 Autonomous Decision AI slot** — up to **6 formulas per model**:

1. `"matchTotal"` — **Match Over/Under (Market 225, incl. OT)**
   - Predicts combined full-game points (`Home + Away`). Valid range: `20` to `400`.
   - CourtQuant compares your exact total against SportyBet `.5` Over/Under ladders and snaps to bookable `.5` lines on the winning side.
2. `"homeTotal"` — **Home Individual Team Over/Under (Market 227, incl. OT)**
   - Predicts Home team full-game points. Valid range: `20` to `200`.
3. `"awayTotal"` — **Away Individual Team Over/Under (Market 228, incl. OT)**
   - Predicts Away team full-game points. Valid range: `20` to `200`.
   - *(Tip: If a model provides both `homeTotal` and `awayTotal`, it can either define `matchTotal` / `handicap` explicitly or let each slot reinforce a coherent scoreboard.)*
4. `"handicap"` — **Handicap / Point Spread (Market 223, incl. OT)**
   - Predicts signed **Home minus Away** point margin (`> 0` means Home favored; `< 0` means Away favored). Valid range: `-75` to `+75`.
   - CourtQuant evaluates Home cover when `projectedMargin + homeHandicapLine > 0` and snaps to offered `.5` spread lines.
5. `"straightWin"` — **Straight Win / Moneyline (Market 219, Winner incl. OT)**
   - Predicts **Home team win probability** as a decimal in `0.001` to `0.999` (`Away win prob = 1 - Home win prob`).
6. `"decision"` — **Autonomous Best-Option Decision AI Formula**
   - For every game, CourtQuant generates candidate betting options across every market that the model supports (Match O/U, Home O/U, Away O/U, Handicap, Straight Win), attaches live SportyBet odds & calibrated win probabilities (`opt.*`), and evaluates your `"decision"` formula on each candidate option.
   - **Highest score = `#1 Best Pick`**, **second highest = `#2`**, **third highest = `#3`** — powering Game Detail Top-3 recommendations and the **Decisions** studio tab (including multi-game slip generation and Respin diversification).

HOW MANY FORMULAS CAN EACH MODEL HAVE?
- **Minimum:** `1` market formula (from slots 1–5 above; unpopulated slots are set to `null`).
- **Full All-Rounder (Recommended default):** All **6 formulas** populated (`5` market formulas + `1` `decision` formula).
- **Market Specialist Option:** In a multi-model pack, you may create 6-formula all-rounders OR specialist models (for example, a Handicap & Straight Win specialist with `"handicap"`, `"straightWin"`, and `"decision"` populated and `"matchTotal": null, "homeTotal": null, "awayTotal": null`). Every model MUST populate **at least 1** market formula (`matchTotal`, `homeTotal`, `awayTotal`, `handicap`, or `straightWin`), and including `"decision"` is strongly recommended so each model controls its own `#1/#2/#3` ranking personality.

- opt.edge — Signed edge in points on the candidate line (or prob margin × 20 for Straight Win)

- opt.prob — Model estimated win probability (0.01–0.99) for this candidate option

- opt.is_away_total — 1 if candidate is Away Team Over/Under (Market 228), else 0
- opt.is_handicap — 1 if candidate is Handicap / Spread (Market 223), else 0
- opt.is_win — 1 if candidate is Straight Win (Market 219), else 0
